Bringing back memories, one song at a time.

This is the heart of Siang Jam. Our mission is to provide music-based intervention for elders to slow cognitive decline and lower the risk of neurological diseases such as Alzheimer’s and dementia.

Volunteer with us Read our story

A message from Meht

Months ago, my grandmother forgot my name, but she could still remember all the songs I played on the piano. Somehow, it had helped her access words and memories that seemed lost.

Alzheimer’s damages the brain in stages. First to go are names and recent events, but the last to go is often musical memory. People with Alzheimer’s are shown to react emotionally to music and learn new songs long after they stop reliably identifying faces.

Combined with the uplifting effects, I realized how powerfully music could positively impact my grandmother, so I started playing to her consistently. Soon, she’d become more present than she’d been in months.

Siang Jam was created so other elders can have that same care. Bringing back memories, one song at a time.

MehtCo-founder
